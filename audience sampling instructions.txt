# Audience Sampling Instructions

The Strudel Dashboard allows you to record custom audio directly from your microphone and instantly use it as an instrument for any of the generative layers (Lead, Bass, Chords, Drums).

## 🎙 How to Record a Sample

1. Locate the **"Sample Recording"** section on the dashboard.
2. Select the **target layer** from the dropdown menu (e.g., "Lead", "Bass", "Chords", "Drums").
3. Click the **🔴 Record** button. 
   - *Note: Your browser may prompt you for permission to use your microphone. You must click "Allow".*
4. Speak, sing, or play an instrument into your microphone!
5. When you're finished, click the **⏹ Stop** button.
6. Wait for the status indicator to show **"Ready."** This means your sample has been uploaded and Strudel has been refreshed.

## 🎧 How to Use Your Sample

Once you have recorded a sample, you need to assign it to the active layer:

1. Locate the **"Instruments"** panel on the dashboard.
2. Find the dropdown for the layer you just recorded to (e.g., "Lead").
3. Scroll to the bottom of the dropdown list and select the `audience_[layer]` option (e.g., `audience_lead`).
4. You will immediately hear your custom sample being used by the generative music engine! 

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

- **"Mic access denied"**: Make sure you clicked "Allow" when the browser asked for microphone permissions. If you accidentally clicked block, look for a camera/microphone icon in your URL bar to reset the permissions.
- **Recording doesn't start**: Make sure you are accessing the dashboard over `localhost` or a secure `https://` connection. Web browsers block microphone access on insecure connections.
- **Instrument isn't in the list**: If the `audience_...` option does not appear in the instrument dropdown after recording, try refreshing the page. The system checks for available samples on load.

## 🗑 Managing & Deleting Samples

All of your recorded audio samples are stored as `.webm` files directly in your project folder. 

**To view or listen to them:**
1. Open the `public/samples/` folder in your project directory. 
2. You will see your recorded files named by the layer and the time they were recorded (e.g., `audience_lead_16938573...webm`). You can listen to these files using any standard media player.

**To delete or reset samples:**
1. Delete the `.webm` audio files from the `public/samples/` directory.
2. Open `public/strudel.json` in your code editor.
3. Remove the filenames from the corresponding arrays (`audience_lead`, `audience_bass`, etc.) so that the arrays are empty `[]` again. 
4. Save `public/strudel.json` and refresh your dashboard.
