# Audience Sampling Instructions

The dashboard records audio from your microphone and turns it into instruments and pad sounds.

## 🎙 How to Record a Sample

1. Find the **"Sample Recording"** panel on the dashboard.
2. Choose what you're recording from the dropdown:
   - **Lead / Bass / Chords / Drums**: a sound to use as a track's instrument.
   - **Lines**: a spoken line from the audience. It goes straight onto pad 1 of the **Lines** pad bank.
   - **Effects**: a verbal sound effect. It goes straight onto pad 1 of the **Effects** pad bank.
3. Click **🔴 Record**. The first time, the browser asks for microphone permission; click **Allow**.
4. Speak, sing or play into the microphone.
5. Click **⏹ Stop**. When the status says **"Saved: <file name>"**, the recording is ready, and it opens in the **Edit Sample** panel.

## ✏️ Edit, Rename or Delete

In **Edit Sample** (use ◀ / ▶ or the list to pick a recording):
- **Trim / fades / ADSR / normalize / tune to C**, then **💾 Apply & Save**. The pads use the edited version straight away.
- **✏️ Rename**: type a name and press Rename or Enter. If the name is taken, the button says "Name taken!".
  **Rename the takes you want to keep.** Unrenamed takes (named like `audience_lines_1791023326331`)
  are treated as likely failed attempts. They're skipped when the pad banks fill up at startup,
  and by the recordings regenerate buttons.
- **🗑️ Delete**: asks for confirmation, then permanently deletes the recording. It's taken off any
  pads (the others close the gap), and any track using it switches back to its default instrument.
  You can delete several bad takes in a row; the editor moves to the next one each time.

## 🎧 How to Use a Recording

- **As a track instrument:** in the **Instruments** panel, open a track's dropdown and pick the recording
  from the "Recorded Audience Samples" group at the bottom, or press **🎙️** for a random recording.
  The "Audience … (Full Bank)" options play all recordings of that type.
- **On the pads:** Lines and Effects recordings land on their pad bank automatically. You can also
  assign any recording to any pad in the Lines / Effects banks with the pad dropdowns.
- **Regenerate buttons:** 🎙️ (recordings), 🗣️ (Lines) and 💥 (Effects) build the whole song from recordings.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

- **"Mic access denied"**: click the camera/microphone icon in the address bar and allow the microphone.
- **Recording doesn't start**: open the dashboard at `http://localhost:3000`. Browsers only allow the
  microphone on `localhost` or `https://`.
- **A recording plays silently as an instrument**: refresh the dashboard.
- **Delete or Rename says the server is out of date**: restart the server (Ctrl + C, then `npm run dev`).

## 🗂 Where Recordings Are Stored

Recordings are `.webm` files in `public/samples/`. The server keeps the catalog `public/strudel.json`
up to date for you. Please don't edit that file or regenerate it with `npx @strudel/sampler`:
that loses which recordings are Lines, Effects and so on. Use the dashboard to add, rename and delete recordings.
