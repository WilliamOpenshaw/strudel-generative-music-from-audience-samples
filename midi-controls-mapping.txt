# M-VAVE SMC-PAD Custom MIDI Mapping

## Pad Bank 1 (Notes 1-16)
- Pad 1 (Note 1): Toggle Drums Mute/Unmute
- Pad 2 (Note 2): Toggle Chords Mute/Unmute
- Pad 3 (Note 3): Toggle Bass Mute/Unmute
- Pad 4 (Note 4): Toggle Melody Mute/Unmute
- Pad 5 (Note 5): Randomize Drums Instrument
- Pad 6 (Note 6): Randomize Chords Instrument
- Pad 7 (Note 7): Randomize Bass Instrument
- Pad 8 (Note 8): Randomize Lead/Melody Instrument
- Pad 9 (Note 9): Regenerate All Layers
- Pad 10 (Note 10): Regenerate Drums Layer
- Pad 11 (Note 11): Regenerate Chords Layer
- Pad 12 (Note 12): Regenerate Bass Layer
- Pad 13 (Note 13): Regenerate Melody Layer
- Pad 14 (Note 14): Pitch Transpose Down
- Pad 15 (Note 15): Pitch Transpose Reset
- Pad 16 (Note 16): Pitch Transpose Up

## Pad Bank 2 (Notes 21-36)
- Pad 1 (Note 21): Reset All Effects
- Pad 2 (Note 22): Reset Drums Effects
- Pad 3 (Note 23): Reset Chords Effects
- Pad 4 (Note 24): Reset Bass Effects
- Pad 5 (Note 25): Reset Melody Effects
- Pad 6 (Note 26): Halve Melody Density
- Pad 7 (Note 27): Double Melody Density
- Pad 8 (Note 28): Halve Bass Density
- Pad 9 (Note 29): Double Bass Density
- Pad 10 (Note 30): Halve Drums Density
- Pad 11 (Note 31): Double Drums Density
- Pad 12 (Note 32): Start Sample Recording
- Pad 13 (Note 33): Stop Sample Recording
- Pad 14-16 (Notes 34-36): Unassigned

## Encoders Bank 1 (CC 1-8)
- Encoder 1 (CC 1): Master Gain
- Encoder 2 (CC 2): Speed (Playback Rate)
- Encoder 3 (CC 3): CPM (Tempo)
- Encoder 4 (CC 4): Transpose
- Encoder 5 (CC 5): Drums Layer Gain
- Encoder 6 (CC 6): Chords Layer Gain
- Encoder 7 (CC 7): Bass Layer Gain
- Encoder 8 (CC 8): Melody Layer Gain

## Encoders Bank 2 (CC 9-16)
- Encoder 1 (CC 9): Drums Room Effect
- Encoder 2 (CC 10): Chords Room Effect
- Encoder 3 (CC 11): Bass Room Effect
- Encoder 4 (CC 12): Melody Room Effect
- Encoder 5 (CC 13): Drums Low-Pass Filter (LPF)
- Encoder 6 (CC 14): Chords Low-Pass Filter (LPF)
- Encoder 7 (CC 15): Bass Low-Pass Filter (LPF)
- Encoder 8 (CC 16): Melody Low-Pass Filter (LPF)

## Transport / Direction Buttons (CC 25-29)
- Left Button (CC 25): Pitch Transpose Down
- Right Button (CC 26): Pitch Transpose Up
- Play Button (CC 27): Start Transport (Play)
- Pause Button (CC 28): Stop Transport (Pause)
- Record Button (CC 29): Toggle Sample Record
