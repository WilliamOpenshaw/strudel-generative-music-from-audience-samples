# M-VAVE SMC-PAD MIDI Mapping

## 16 Pads Mapping
*Assumed Notes 36 to 51 based on typical configuration*

**Original 8 Pads (Notes 36-43)**
- Pad 1 (Note 36): Toggle Drums Mute/Unmute
- Pad 2 (Note 37): Toggle Chords Mute/Unmute
- Pad 3 (Note 38): Toggle Bass Mute/Unmute
- Pad 4 (Note 39): Toggle Melody Mute/Unmute
- Pad 5 (Note 40): Regenerate All Layers
- Pad 6 (Note 41): Regenerate Chords Layer
- Pad 7 (Note 42): Regenerate Bass Layer
- Pad 8 (Note 43): Regenerate Melody Layer

**Extra 8 Pads (Notes 44-51)**
- Pad 9 (Note 44): Regenerate Drums Layer
- Pad 10 (Note 45): Start Transport (Play)
- Pad 11 (Note 46): Stop Transport
- Pad 12 (Note 47): Randomize Drums Instrument
- Pad 13 (Note 48): Randomize Chords Instrument
- Pad 14 (Note 49): Randomize Bass Instrument
- Pad 15 (Note 50): Randomize Lead/Melody Instrument
- Pad 16 (Note 51): Stop Transport (Redundant/Fallback)

## 16 Endless Encoders Mapping
*Mapped to CC 1 through 12*

**First 4 Encoders (CC 1-4)**
- Encoder 1 (CC 1): Master Gain
- Encoder 2 (CC 2): Speed (Playback Rate)
- Encoder 3 (CC 3): CPM (Tempo)
- Encoder 4 (CC 4): Transpose

**Next 4 Encoders (CC 5-8)**
- Encoder 5 (CC 5): Drums Layer Gain
- Encoder 6 (CC 6): Chords Layer Gain
- Encoder 7 (CC 7): Bass Layer Gain
- Encoder 8 (CC 8): Melody Layer Gain

**Next 4 Encoders (CC 9-12)**
- Encoder 9 (CC 9): Chords Low-Pass Filter (LPF)
- Encoder 10 (CC 10): Chords Room Effect
- Encoder 11 (CC 11): Bass Low-Pass Filter (LPF)
- Encoder 12 (CC 12): Melody Delay Effect

*Note: Depending on your hardware editor, you may need to assign these MIDI Notes (36-51) and CCs (1-12) to the physical pads and encoders of your M-VAVE SMC-PADs using their configuration software.*
