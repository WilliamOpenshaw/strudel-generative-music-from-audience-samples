# M-VAVE SMC-PAD MIDI Mapping (current)

Source of truth: CC_MAP, PAD_MAP and CC_BUTTON_MAP at the top of src/midi/midi.js.
Edit those to change the mapping; the dashboard picks up changes on refresh.
The "Last MIDI" line under the dashboard header shows what each press did.

The controller has two pad modes: one sends notes 1-16, the other notes 21-36.

## Pads, notes 21-36 (the pad banks): physical pads 1-16
- Pads 1-15 (notes 21-35): play pad 1-15 of the active pad bank
  (Lines / Effects one-shots, or Notes / Sample Notes chord notes;
  in the Notes banks pad 14 picks the chord and pad 15 changes the instrument or sample)
- Pad 16 (note 36): switch to the next pad bank that is turned on

## Pads, notes 1-16
- Pad 1 (note 1): mute/unmute Drums
- Pad 2 (note 2): mute/unmute Chords
- Pad 3 (note 3): mute/unmute Bass
- Pad 4 (note 4): mute/unmute Melody
- Pad 5 (note 5): random Drums instrument
- Pad 6 (note 6): random Chords instrument
- Pad 7 (note 7): random Bass instrument
- Pad 8 (note 8): random Lead/Melody instrument
- Pad 9 (note 9): Regenerate all
- Pad 10 (note 10): New drums
- Pad 11 (note 11): New chords
- Pad 12 (note 12): New bass
- Pad 13 (note 13): New melody
- Pads 14-16 (notes 14-16): do nothing at the moment (they were global transpose
  down / reset / up, which was replaced by per-track Pitch controls)

## Knobs (encoders), first set: CC 1-8
- CC 1: Music volume (generated music only)
- CC 2: Speed
- CC 3: CPM (tempo)
- CC 4: not mapped
- CC 5: Drums volume
- CC 6: Chords volume
- CC 7: Bass volume
- CC 8: Melody volume

## Knobs (encoders), second set: CC 9-16
- CC 9: Drums reverb (room)
- CC 10: Chords reverb (room)
- CC 11: Bass reverb (room)
- CC 12: Melody reverb (room)
- CC 13: Drums low-pass filter
- CC 14: Chords low-pass filter
- CC 15: Bass low-pass filter
- CC 16: Melody low-pass filter

## Transport buttons
- Play (CC 27): Start
- Pause (CC 28): Stop
- Record (CC 29): start / stop a sample recording
- Left / Right (CC 25 / 26): not mapped

## Host Messages page
While the Host Messages page (host.html) is started, it takes over ALL pads in both
note ranges: any pad plays the next message, pad 13 goes back, pad 16 skips forward.
The dashboard ignores pad presses until you press Stop there. Knobs and transport
buttons keep working on the dashboard.

## Pad and MIDI volume
Pad sounds you play (MIDI or on-screen) use the dashboard's "MIDI pads" volume slider.
