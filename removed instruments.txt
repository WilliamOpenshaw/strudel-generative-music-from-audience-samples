REMOVED INSTRUMENTS
===================

Date: 2026-10-03

These built-in instruments were removed from the Melody (Lead), Bass and Chords
instrument dropdowns because they were silent or slow to start.

How they were tested: each instrument was played on the lead track (other tracks off)
and the audio output was measured. "Slow" means more than 1.5 seconds from
selecting the instrument to its first sound. For comparison, a basic synth starts
in about 0.6 seconds. Most removed instruments download their audio from the
internet (GitHub) the first time they play, which is slow or unreliable on venue Wi-Fi.

Totals: 117 removed (52 silent, 65 slow). 173 kept.
  Basic synths:        4 of 4 kept
  Strudel synths:      21 of 21 kept
  General MIDI:        95 of 125 kept (30 removed)
  Sampled instruments: 53 of 140 kept (87 removed)
  Drum kits:           not tested, unchanged

Notes pad bank (bank 3)
-----------------------
The same rule was applied to the instruments the Notes bank picks from (pad 15).
Removed from that list: gm_piano, gm_music_box, gm_vibraphone, gm_xylophone, gm_steel_drums, gm_dulcimer, gm_acoustic_guitar_steel, gm_recorder, gm_accordion.
Its default instrument changed from gm_piano (2.5 s) to gm_epiano1 (1.1 s).

Restoring an instrument
-----------------------
Add its name back to the matching list in src/soundfonts/strudel-instruments.js
("gm" for General MIDI, "dirtSamples" for sampled instruments). The previous full
lists are in git history. Storing the samples locally (after the show) would make
most of the slow ones fast again.
